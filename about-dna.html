◉ Charter §0.3 · The genome

The four properties, stated as tests.

Each property is written here the way it is enforced — as a condition a page must satisfy before it is permitted to exist, not as a virtue the author is asked to remember.

⏳

Decay-aware

Every substantive claim carries a class — Durable, Semi-durable, or Perishable — and a review interval implied by that class. A page whose review interval has elapsed is marked stale on its own face rather than silently trusted. Nothing here pretends to be permanent.

⊘

Falsifiable

Every substantive claim carries an explicit null, the disconfirming evidence where it exists, and a decision rule. A claim that cannot be wrong is not knowledge and does not ship.

⚖

Governed

An ethics reviewer with genuine veto exists before the first publication, not after the first incident. Governance is architecture here, not remediation.

⎇

Version-controlled

No page changes without a changelog entry. This is the axiom. It is enforced mechanically by static hosting, which makes silent revision technically impossible rather than merely discouraged.

→ Why this is mechanical

Trust is a property of the architecture, not the author.

Everything a visitor reads is a file in a repository. Changing a file requires a commit, a review and a deployment — and each of those leaves a record that cannot be removed without leaving a further record.

▤

A file, not a database row

Because pages are static files under version control, there is no admin panel that can quietly rewrite history. The commit log is the audit trail, and it is public.

↺

A diff, not a decree

A change you cannot see is a change that did not happen. Every substantive edit ships with a changelog entry and a diff a reader can inspect.

◐

A defect, not a secret

Every dependency that has not yet been engineered away is a defect, and defects in this universe are published rather than managed — with an owner and a date.

⊘ Charter §0.4 · Non-negotiable

Three things the DNA refuses absolutely.

These refusals bind every pillar, every academy and every agent. They admit of no research exception and no contextual reframing, and §0.4 may not be amended to weaken them.

🚫

Laboratory instruction in biology

No wet-lab protocols, no substrate cultivation, no synthetic-biology bench instruction, no organism engineering. The human-cell exclusion is stated in full wherever bio-adjacent material appears and is never paraphrased, summarised, or collapsed into an accordion.

🚫

Core Riba

As defined in §C.9.2 of Maher's License — refused in the operation of MetaX's own finances, in the design of any Work it publishes, and as a condition of academy membership.

🚫

Sexualisation of minors

In any content, in any language, in any framing. MetaX refuses to produce, host, or tolerate material that facilitates the grooming, isolation, or exploitation of children. No research exception. No artistic exception.
