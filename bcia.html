◉ What it is · and is not

Precision before ambition.

✔

What BCIA is

A disciplined, decay-aware study of information architecture in living and embodied substrates — with governance built in from the first line, and every claim dated and falsifiable.

✕

What BCIA is not

It is not a claim that biology beats silicon today, not human-cell research, and not a marketing story. The DishBrain-vs-DeepMind "28,800×" style like-for-like efficiency claim is banned as a category error.
